Private personal integration

Personal Workspace Connector

This application is a private productivity integration used only by its owner. It connects the owner's Google account to automation running on systems controlled by the owner.

What it does

The connector helps its owner review and organize email, manage calendars, and work with selected Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets content. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept public registrations.

Data handling

Google account data is processed only to perform actions requested or configured by the account owner. The application does not sell personal data, serve advertising, or use Google account data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.
